* Graph Algorithms
1) Some basic knowledge and conventions about graph in CS.
2) Two basic algorithms used. Most others are based on them.
3) Two simple applications.

* Basis
- Graph: /directed/ and /undirected/
- Storage: /adjacency list/ or /adjacency matrix/
- The most important two things of a graph: V and E.
- List is more space-efficient than Matrix(at most asymptotically equal). But we prefer later, because it's simpler and more efficient and, in unweighted graph we just need one bit to store one edge.
